Something broke? What the store has to do

Short answers to the things stores tell you when you bring a faulty item back, with the section of the Australian Consumer Law for each.

  • "It's out of warranty"
    The warranty isn't the limit. Goods have to last a reasonable time.
  • "Take it up with the manufacturer"
    Your claim is with the store that sold it to you.
  • "No receipt, no refund"
    A bank statement or order email counts as proof of purchase.
